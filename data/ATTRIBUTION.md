# Source attribution

- World overview: Natural Earth v5.1.2, 1:110m Admin 0 countries, public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . The bundled overview retains the source's generalized geometry, boundaries and English names; it is not a street map or routing database. Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Original SHA-256: `6866c877d39cba9c357620878839b336d569f8c662d3cfab4cb1dbe2d39c977f`. Regenerate the compact asset with `node scripts/world-map.mjs`; ordinary builds require no world-data download.

- Map geometry and roads: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Optional campus enrichment: Overture Maps Foundation, https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/ . Imports retain dataset/provider, record ID, release, retrieval receipt and per-field evidence. Places use the applicable provider's CDLA Permissive 2.0, Apache 2.0 or CC0 terms; building data uses ODbL and applicable upstream attribution. Unknown/changed provider licenses are held for review. Required CDLA/Apache text and Foursquare notices are retained in `data/licenses/` and embedded in source metadata for offline display. Foursquare data is transformed by Overture and converted into TurnRight campus fields. Google Open Buildings, when explicitly identified by Overture, is separately licensed CC BY 4.0 data; it is not Google Maps content. Initial candidates do not constitute approved or published campus additions.
- LASU campus layers: MangroveandpartnersLimited, LASU Webmap – Main, ArcGIS item `ffd68667b1464eeb999c0050897a82a0`. Item metadata was modified June 2023; that is not a survey date. The public item supplied no explicit redistribution license. On 8 September 2026, the TurnRight project owner confirmed that permission covers offline redistribution. This records the owner's confirmation; the underlying agreement was not independently inspected. Retain the permission correspondence with the project records. ArcGIS Online terms: https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-online/reference/terms-of-use.htm
- The raw source layers remain individually identified by source IDs; corrections retain provenance. The downloadable JSON contains the OSM-derived database. No Google Maps content, satellite imagery, or OSM rendered tiles are bundled.
- Student walking permission: on 9 September 2026 the project owner confirmed that ordinary internal campus roads are open to students except for specific restricted areas. `data/campus-access.json` records the reviewed road IDs. Original OSM access tags are retained alongside the correction; this does not establish unrestricted public access or field verification. See `docs/CAMPUS-ACCESS.md`.
- Individual connection confirmations: on 9 September 2026 the owner separately confirmed student walking through the Faculty of Law driveway (`osm:way:591396656`) and International Library gate (`osm:node:13774055448`). Their `access=private` source tags remain unchanged. Each has its own dated review record; the allowance does not extend to other driveways or gates. See `docs/CONNECTION-REVIEW.md`.

- Interface font: Inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1, via @fontsource-variable/inter.
- Natural navigation recordings: generated locally with hexgrad Kokoro v1.0 (`Kokoro-82M`), British female `bf_emma`, using Kokoro 0.9.4. The model declares Apache License 2.0; see [model card](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), [voice documentation](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/blob/main/VOICES.md), and [retained license text](voice/KOKORO-LICENSE.txt). TurnRight supplies the direction text and the published campus vocabulary. Only generated MP3s and their manifest ship to users; model/runtime dependencies stay on the maintenance machine. Revision, SHA-256 checksums, source map version and pronunciation overrides are recorded in `data/voice/` and the voice manifest. [Regeneration and limitations](../docs/VOICE.md).
- Legacy navigation recordings: generated locally using Windows speech synthesis (Microsoft Hazel), retained unchanged in campus packages as fallback. No online speech service or system speech synthesis is used during navigation.

This map has not been field surveyed by TurnRight. A mapped approach ends on a source path near a building and does not imply an entrance, access permission, or a walkable final connection. Approximate 3D heights use documented floor descriptions at 3 metres per floor.
